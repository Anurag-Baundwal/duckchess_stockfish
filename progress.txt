2026-10-31  Analysis and decisions (before the repo existed)
  - Wrote ..\duck_engine_analysis.md. Main conclusions:
    * Fork Stockfish for its infrastructure (UCI, TT, threads, time
      management, bench, tune.h, Makefile/PGO), not for its search. Strip the
      search to a minimal baseline and bring features back one SPRT at a time,
      because many of Stockfish's tweaks assume ~35 moves per node (e.g.
      `r -= moveCount * 65` and the LMP move-count limit break at ~1000).
    * wakwak (5331 COPE, 8 weeks old) gets its strength from duck-specific
      pruning (duck refutations, Duck or Die, neutral ducks, caps per chess
      move and per duck square, duckless TT, duck-aware SEE) plus a large
      NNUE. Most of it is common sense; refutations and duckless hashing are
      the genuinely inventive parts.
    * A turn is one combined move (chess move + duck square), as in wakwak and
      Fairy-Stockfish. Split plies were rejected: Stockfish's stack assumes
      ss-1 is the opponent's move.
    * Testing: OpenBench needs a per-variant runner (port MattBench 213d671)
      with Silverrzz/fastchess `-variant duck`. That is a later, separate plan.
  - Reference perft: Fairy-Stockfish duck build
    repos7/Fairy-Stockfish/src/fsf-duck.exe (needs /c/msys64/clang64/bin on
    PATH for libc++.dll). Startpos perft 1 = 640, perft 2 = 379440.

2026-10-31  1.3a Remove universal binary builds                (this commit)
  - Universal builds (ARCH=x86-64-universal, arm64-universal,
    riscv64-universal, and macos-lipo) produce one binary with every x86/ARM
    variant and the NNUE net embedded, picking the best at runtime. OpenBench
    builds natively on each worker, so they add nothing here, and they cannot
    work once the net is gone.
  - Deleted src/universal/, scripts/check_universal*.sh, the Makefile's
    Section 6 (universal driver), the universal-object-* and macos-lipo
    targets, universalclean, the UNIVERSAL_BUILD switches, the universal
    ARCH names and help lines, and main.cpp's UNIVERSAL_BINARY entry shim.
    .gitignore lost the universal entries.
  - Kept: the mingw `-save-temps` link flag. It predates universal builds
    (Stockfish commit 67062637, Windows native builds).
  - src/nnue/network.cpp still has dead UNIVERSAL_BINARY #ifdefs; the whole
    file goes in the next commit.
  - Verified: clean rebuild without warnings; Bench 1556650 (unchanged);
    `make help` lists no universal targets.
